## My Favorite Anime Series: Haikyuu!!

**Genre:** Sports, Comedy, Coming of Age, Slice of Life, Drama
**Release:** 2014-Present

### Description

*Haikyuu!!* is a volleyball-centric anime based on the manga with the same name by Haruichi Furudate. It follows **Shoyo Hinata**, a short boy who becomes inspired to play volleyball after seeing a small but powerful player who later then became his inspiration and role model known as the "Little Giant." Even though he lacks the height, Hinata works hard to prove he can still soar above the net, just like his idol.

In high school, he joins **Karasuno High School's** men volleyball club and ends up teaming up with his rival, the talented setter **Tobio Kageyama**, who he promised to beat before back in middle school. Together they build an unstoppable duo while the team fights to return to the national stage and reclaim their legacy as one of Japan's Volleyball powerhouse.

### Main Characters

### Karasuno High School

**First Year Players**
1. Shoyo Hinata - a short but energetic player who jumps as high as his dreams of someday playing for his country to the nationals, I see him as a ball of sunshine who pursues his dreams 
2. Tobio Kageyama - the genius setter given the title of "King of The Court" by his middle school teammates not because of his great skills but mocking him as tyrannical, I believed that he is somewhat the most misunderstood character
3. Kei Tsukishima - a tall, sarcastic middle blocker who grows along with his passion for volleyball throughout the series
4. Tadashi Yamaguchi - Tsukishima's timid best friend and a pinch server who learns to work on his self-confidence

**Second Year Players**
1. Ryunosuke Tanaka - Nishinoya's best friend and the loud and passionate wing spiker of Karasuno
2. Yu Nishinoya - the fearless libero, entitled as "Karasuno's Guardian Deity" because of for his amazing saves and receives 
3. Chikara Ennoshita - a calm and dependable wing spiker who later becomes captain
4. Kazuhito Narita - a hardworking wing spiker and a supportive teammate
5. Hisashi Kinoshita - a cheerful wing spiker who always backs up the team

**Third Year Players**
1. Daichi Sawamura - the calm and reliable captain of Karasuno
2. Koshi Sugawara - the kind and supportive vice-captain and setter of Karasuno who puts the dream of his teammates and chose to let Kageyama play in his place because he believed they would reach the nationals with their skills and talent
3. Asahi Azumane - the ace with a powerful spike but gentle heart

**Managers**
1. Kiyoko Shimizu - the cool, pretty, and elegant third-year manager

**Coaches**
1. Keishin Ukai - the head coach, a former Karasuno player who works at his family's shop
2. Ittetsu Takeda - the teacher and advisor who works hard to support the team

### What I Enjoy About It

1. **Classism and Social Inequality** – It shows how opportunities, resources, and privileges can influence someone's chances of success. Not everyone starts on equal footing, but that doesn't mean those with less have no place among the best.

2. **Pursuing Your Dreams Despite Limitations** – Through Hinata, Hoshiumi, and The "Little Giant", the series teaches that being small, inexperienced, or underestimated shouldn't stop you from chasing something you genuinely love.

3. **Talent Isn't Everything** – Natural ability can give someone a head start (like Kageyama), but discipline, hard work, consistency, and willingness to improve can take you just as far (like Oikawa).

4. **Everyone Has Their Own Pace** – Not everyone develops at the same speed. Some people discover their strengths early, while others take longer to find their place and potential, like Yamaguchi. 

5. **The Importance of Teamwork** – Individual talent is valuable, but learning to trust others, communicate, and work toward a shared goal is what makes a team stronger.

6. **Healthy Competition** – Your rivals aren't necessarily your enemies. Sometimes, the people you compete against are also the ones who inspire you to become better. Just like how players like Kuroo supported and inspired Tsukishima to find his love for Volleyball.

7. **Failure Is Part of Growth** – Losing isn't always a sign that you aren't good enough. Sometimes, it is an opportunity to recognize your weaknesses and understand what you need to work on.

8. **There Is No Such Thing as a Small Role** – Not everyone has to be the star or the main player. Haikyuu shows that every individual has something valuable to contribute, even if their efforts aren't always recognized. Just like how Sugawara, Kinoshita, Ennoshita, and Narita supported the main players even though they also wanted to shine. 

9. **The Reality of Outgrowing Your Dreams** – It explores how people change, how circumstances don't always go as planned, and how letting go of something doesn't necessarily mean you've failed.

10. **Finding Purpose Beyond Winning** – The journey, friendships, experiences, and lessons you gain along the way can be just as meaningful as achieving your original goal.

11. **Respecting Your Opponents** – Every opposing team has its own struggles, ambitions, and reasons for playing. It reminds us that everyone is the protagonist of their own story. Despite rivalries, there was never a disrespect shown from players to other players.

12. **Making the Most of the Present** – Nothing lasts forever. School life, friendships, opportunities, and even the moments we take for granted eventually come to an end. That's why we should appreciate them while we can.

### Notable Lines to Live By

> "Fly."
This is Karasuno's motto, written on the team banner. It represents the team rising back to the top after being labeled as "Flightless Crows" after their fall from the top.

> "You have to be able to rely on others."
Independence is important, but so is allowing yourself to receive support. You don't always have to be strong on your own.

> "The moment you think of giving up, think of the reason why you held on so long."
There will be moments when your goals become exhausting and your motivation disappears. Remembering why you started can help you rediscover the courage to continue.

> "Being weak means that there is room to grow."
Acknowledging your weaknesses isn't embarrassing. It means you understand that you still have something to work on.

> "The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams."
You don't have to be the most talented or naturally gifted person in the room. Sometimes, the willingness to keep pursuing something despite the odds is what sets you apart.

> "Everyone has their own way of playing."
People have different strengths, circumstances, and ways of achieving their goals. There isn't one universal path to success.

> "Talent is something you make bloom. Instinct is something you polish."
Some people are naturally gifted, while others have to work harder to reach the same level. But talent alone does not guarantee success. Potential needs effort, discipline, and development.

> "There is no such thing as a worthless challenge."
Even when you fail, attempting something difficult is never a waste. Every challenge teaches you something about yourself.

> "When you're feeling down, look up!"
Your perspective can change the way you approach difficulties. You don't have to let one bad experience define your entire journey.

> "If you want to change, you have to take the first step."
Waiting until you're confident enough can keep you stuck. Sometimes, you have to act while you're still afraid.
